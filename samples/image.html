Images

Raster images (PNG / JPEG / GIF / BMP) are embedded as real PDF image objects, so they stay crisp and non-selectable. Sizes are resolved at layout time from the file header, and the intrinsic aspect ratio is always preserved. Images are block-level: place them as their own element and align them with text-align.

1. Full intrinsic size (auto-capped to the page)

No width/height: the 600×260 logo renders at its native 450×195 pt, shrunk to fit the content width.

<img src="assets/logo.png">

2. Width only — height follows the aspect ratio

Give only width="240"; the height is derived (240 × 260/600 ≈ 104 pt).

<img src="assets/logo.png" width="240">

3. Alignment

A block image honours its own text-align within the content area.

centered and right-aligned copies

4. Border and explicit box

Both dimensions fixed, with a ruled frame drawn around the image.
