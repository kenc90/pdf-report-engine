Chart depth

Three additions to the vector chart family: 100% stacked bars, combo charts with a secondary axis, and waterfall bridges. All are still plain draw commands — vector, paginated and dependency-free.

Combo + secondary axis

Revenue bars read the left axis in millions; the margin line reads the right axis in percent. data-series-types and data-series-axes are aligned with data-series by index.

100% stacked bars

data-stacked="100%" normalises each category to its own total, so the value axis reads 0–100% and the mix is comparable across years with different revenue.

Waterfall

data-totals="0,5" marks the starting and ending balances; the bars in between are deltas, connected by dashed running-total lines.
