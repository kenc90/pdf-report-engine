Document Watermark

This document has a declarative DRAFT watermark rotated diagonally across every page. The watermark is specified via a <meta> tag in the head.

Page 1 Content

The watermark is rendered behind all content with 18% opacity and -35° rotation, making it clearly visible without obscuring the text. It repeats on every generated page.
