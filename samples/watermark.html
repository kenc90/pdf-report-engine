Document Watermark

This document has a declarative DRAFT watermark painted diagonally across every page. It is specified via a <meta name="watermark"> tag in the head, uses data-watermark-layer="foreground" to sit on top of the body text, and is rendered as filled vector glyph outlines so it is not selectable.

Page 1 Content

The watermark is drawn with 28% opacity and -35° rotation. Because it uses the foreground layer it paints after the header, body and footer of every page. Change the attribute to background (or drop it entirely) to place it behind content instead.
