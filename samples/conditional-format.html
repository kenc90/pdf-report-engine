Conditional formatting

Three declarative layers: data-when (conditional style), data-if (conditional visibility) and data-color-scale (numeric heatmaps). Every condition runs through the same sandboxed expression scope as data-bind, so it can read $row, $group, $index and $data.

Block-level conditions too. This callout is only painted while $data.accounts.length is between 6 and 9, and the amber border is applied by data-when.
