Merged Cells

Both horizontal merges (colspan) and vertical merges (rowspan) are supported, and they can be combined in a single table. Vertical merges are honored in authored rows (headers, footers and literal body rows).

1. Vertical merge — rowspan

Each region label spans three month rows and is centered vertically across the merged cell.

Region Month Revenue
North January 120,000
February 131,500
March 142,000
South January 90,200
February 95,800
March 110,400

Table 1 — a rowspan="3" label cell centered over its group.

2. Horizontal merge — colspan

The header row defines the column grid; body cells then span groups of those columns.

# Item Qty Unit Total
1 Cloud hosting 12 400.00 4,800.00
2 Support plan 1 1,200.00 1,200.00
Subtotal (before tax) 6,000.00
Payment is due within 30 days of invoice date

Table 2 — a colspan="4" subtotal and a colspan="5" full-width banner.

3. Both together — rowspan + colspan

A day label spans two time rows (rowspan), while a session title spans two columns (colspan).

Day Slot Session Room
Monday 09:00 Opening keynote — all attendees
11:00 Layout engines Hall A
Tuesday 09:00 Pagination deep-dive Hall B
14:00 Closing panel & Q&A

Table 3 — combined merges: a two-row day cell and full-width / two-column session cells in one grid.
